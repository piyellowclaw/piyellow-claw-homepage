# Computational portrait / 一隻小爪，一個活著的程式

Cream paper, ink, acid yellow and a restrained coral accent frame an oversized,
legible name. Tracked monospaced labels and thin rules give the page the rhythm
of a gallery publication. Capabilities become ruled editorial rows; the supplied
mascot and studio illustrations retain their own space and original rights.

The original Canvas2D sketch grows three contour branches into an orbital palm
and sun. A seeded generator sets branch reach and curvature; recursive rectangles
expose a small specimen of the subdivision system. Density changes actual line
count. Pointer proximity gently bends branches. Regeneration changes the seed,
not a simulated metric. This is a local sketch, not live AI telemetry.

The default is static. Optional breathing is bounded to two drawing units and
capped at 12.5 draws per second; DPR is capped at 1.5 and width at 1600 pixels.
Animation stops offscreen and in hidden tabs. Reduced motion disables breathing
and pointer response. Native buttons and range input work by keyboard, with
localized status, visible focus, and DOM explanation including a no-script fallback.
Both languages are complete documents; storage is optional.

## Interpretation and references

- [Newyellow homepage](https://newyellow.xyz/): whitespace, tracked headings and a full-width artwork become an editorial frame rather than a conventional product hero.
- [Succulent (2023)](https://newyellow.xyz/en/works/2023-succulent): recursive subdivision and layered growth inform a bounded branching system. The program is a gardener of constraints rather than a source of arbitrary particles.
- [Resonance Like Water (2022)](https://newyellow.xyz/en/works/2022-resonance-like-water): interwoven lines and changing representation inform the density and pointer interactions. Here the input is explicitly local parameters, not external data.

Visual references include Succulent’s museum presentation and recursively
layered leaves, and Resonance’s black field of interwoven waveforms and data
dots. These inform the system’s principles, not a reproduction of either work.
No reference assets, artwork or code were copied. Existing MIT source and artwork
exclusions remain in LICENSE and assets/NOTICE.md. The build allowlist is unchanged.
